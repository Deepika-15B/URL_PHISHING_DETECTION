## Impact Assessment

If feature vector duplicates exist across train and test splits,
the model may have memorized those exact patterns, potentially inflating
test metrics. With the processed_v2 dataset (which used URL-level dedup),
the remaining duplicates are from genuinely different URLs that happen
to produce identical feature vectors.
